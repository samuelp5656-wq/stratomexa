Scope of Deliverables

Exact deliverables managed under this service agreement.

Annual ROC compliance (Form AOC-4 financial statements, MGT-7/7A annual returns)
Director compliance: annual DIR-3 KYC, MBP-1 disclosure, and DIR-8 qualification filings
Foreign Direct Investment reporting on the RBI FIRMS portal (Form FC-GPR / FC-TRS)
Annual Foreign Liabilities and Assets (FLA) return filing with Reserve Bank of India
Statutory Registers maintenance (Register of Members, Directors, Charges, and Share Transfers)

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the annual FLA return required to be filed with RBI?

Companies that have received FDI or made ODI must submit the annual FLA return by July 15th every year based on audited or provisional financials.

What happens if an MCA annual filing like AOC-4 is delayed?

MCA levies severe late fees of Rs. 100 per day per form with no upper cap, along with potential prosecution notices for directors. Our compliance calendar prevents these delays.
